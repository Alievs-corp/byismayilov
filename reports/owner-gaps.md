# Owner gaps

Facts the salon owner must supply. Nothing below is invented on the site; each item is hidden or shown as an admin-editable empty field until filled.

## Contact (`/elaqe`, footer, structured data)

- Working hours (Admin, Settings, working hours text, and the structured `openingHours` value): not published anywhere.
- WhatsApp number: not published; the WhatsApp row stays hidden until it is set.
- Map coordinates (latitude and longitude) or a Google Maps embed link: the map card currently opens a Google Maps search for the address.
- Storefront photo and landmark directions for the "how to find us" block: there is no admin field for a storefront photo yet; the block shows the address only.
- Email address: not published.

## Masters (`/ustalar`)

- Other team members: only the account owner is known. Each master needs a name, role, short bio, photo and Instagram link.
- The list of services each master performs (linked per master in Admin, Masters).

## Gallery (`/islerimiz`)

- Category for every work item (balayage, blonde, ombre, cut, salon) so the filter chips appear.
- Real before and after pairs (two photos of the same client, with consent).
- Captions per photo in az, ru and en.

## Blog and pages

- At least one real article before the blog is indexed; the empty state is shown until then.
- Privacy policy and any other legal page text.
